To my favorite human,

I am glad I get to know you in the ordinary moments, too. The conversations, the small kindnesses, and the days when simply being together is enough.

May the year ahead make room for your dreams. Wherever it takes you, I hope you carry this with you: you matter to me, and I am cheering for you.
